The artist behind the artistry

Meet Sukh.

I'm Sukh, a 34-year-old male makeup artist based in Edmonton, Alberta, and a Canadian-educated beauty professional. My fascination with makeup began in childhood. What started as a genuine love for colour, detail and transformation has grown into a craft I continue to refine with care, creativity and purpose.

I specialize in creating looks that feel considered and personal — from timeless bridal and engagement makeup to reception, party, HD, glam, creative and editorial artistry. I believe great makeup should complement the person wearing it, photograph beautifully and feel comfortable throughout the occasion.

As a mobile makeup artist, I travel to your getting-ready location in Edmonton and surrounding areas. My goal is simple: to make your day feel special, seamless and perfect, from our first conversation to the final touch.
